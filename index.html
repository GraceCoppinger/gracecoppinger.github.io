About Me

I am a PhD student in Ecology & Evolutionary Biology at Washington University in St. Louis. My research explores how climate change and urbanization influence plant–pathogen interactions across spatial and temporal scales.

I am broadly interested in disease ecology, evolutionary ecology, population genetics, and conservation.

Research

Climate Change & Disease

Investigating how changing temperature regimes influence plant pathogens and disease dynamics.

Urban Ecology

Exploring how urbanization alters plant habitat, disease prevalence, and ecological interactions.

Population Genomics

Using genomic approaches to understand population structure and evolutionary change in plant pathogens.
